tiny photo booth

A wall-mounted photo strip machine for dive bars — tap, pose, print.

The product

tiny photo booth is a compact, wall-mounted unit that takes four photos and prints a black-and-white strip on the spot. Built for bars: small footprint, fast print, auto-cut, and a one-button flow that fits the room without a full rental booth.

Guests see sample strips and a clear price, press Start, pay on a certified card reader, then get a countdown and a cut thermal strip in hand. No ink cartridges, no attendant, no floor space eaten by a trailer booth — just a glowing frame on the wall and a strip worth keeping for the night.

V1 prints on 80 mm thermal paper (~2.8 × 8 in strip) with a grainy photo-booth look. Price point: $5 per strip.
